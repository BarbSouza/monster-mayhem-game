📖 How to Play

Objective: Eliminate your opponent by having 10 of their monsters removed.

Each turn you can:

  • Place one new monster on your edge row
  • Move any of your existing monsters once each

Movement rules:

  • Monsters move any number of squares horizontally or vertically
  • Monsters move up to 2 squares diagonally
  • Monsters can pass over your own monsters but cannot land on them
  • Monsters cannot enter the opponent's starting row

Combat — when two monsters land on the same square:

  • 🧛 Vampire beats 🐺 Werewolf
  • 🐺 Werewolf beats 👻 Ghost
  • 👻 Ghost beats 🧛 Vampire
  • Same type? Both are removed!

⚡ Turns are simultaneous! Both players move at the same time — neither player sees the opponent's moves until both press End Turn. Plan carefully!
